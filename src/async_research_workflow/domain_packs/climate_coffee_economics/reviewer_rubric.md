# Climate Coffee Economics Reviewer Rubric

Use this rubric for paired review of generic baseline, upgraded runtime, human
baseline, or permissioned Deep Research-style outputs. Reviewers should not see
another reviewer score before submitting their own notes.

## Required Scores

Score each category from 1 to 5 and cite the artifact path or evidence id that
supports the score.

| Category | Score 1 | Score 5 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief fit | Output misses the bounded coffee economics question. | Output answers the brief, source policy, and audience constraints. |
| Groundedness | Material claims rely on model memory or missing citations. | Material claims map to evidence spans or computed artifacts. |
| Source policy compliance | Private, stale, or unlicensed sources are reused unsafely. | Source class, license/use, freshness, and private/public limits are explicit. |
| Empirical discipline | Numeric or causal claims are asserted without checks. | Computations are reproducible and linked to input artifacts. |
| Deliverable maturity | Output overstates readiness or hides gaps. | Output states maturity, open gaps, response obligations, and human gates. |
| Cost and latency | Costs or latency are missing or unjustified. | Costs and latency are recorded and proportionate to accepted output. |

## Preference Rule

Choose a winner only when the score difference is material and the higher score
does not depend on unsupported or stale claims. Otherwise mark the pair as
`tie` or `unproven`.

## Mandatory Caveats

- Do not infer broad Deep Research-style product superiority from this pack.
- Mark proprietary product comparisons `unproven` unless the report includes
  product, capture date, prompt, source permissions, and reviewer calibration.
- Treat private/local buyer evidence as non-public unless the task contract
  records publication permission.
