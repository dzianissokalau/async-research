# Known Data Gaps

Data limitations that block or constrain the real-estate worked example.

| gap_id | status | affected_items | data_needed | blocker | next_step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DG-0001 | open | repeat-sales experiments | stable property identity or documented matching method | Price Paid Data does not provide a stable property ID | create a data-readiness task for matching method and licensing review |
| DG-0002 | open | mortgage-rate shock analysis | borrower-level mortgage terms | Bank of England series are aggregate rates only | limit claims to macro context unless borrower-level data is approved |
| DG-0003 | open | local-area feature joins | approved geography lookup and address/postcode methodology | address-derived outputs can have licensing and privacy constraints | audit the geography source before experiment planning |

## Notes

Gaps should feed idea scoring and planner task selection. Do not treat a gap as
resolved until a reviewed task or human decision records the resolution.
